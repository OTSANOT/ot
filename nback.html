How Dual N-Back Works

You will be presented with a sequence of stimuli: a lit square on the 3×3 grid and a spoken letter.

Your goal is to press Position or Audio if the current stimulus matches the one presented N steps earlier in the sequence.

Example (2-Back):
Step 1: Top-Left + "C"
Step 2: Center + "K"
Step 3: Top-Left + "R" → POSITION MATCH!

Tip: Both Position and Audio can match simultaneously on the same turn!
