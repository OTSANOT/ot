Inhibition Control

Inhibitory control is the ability to stop an automatic or inappropriate response. In this Go/No-Go exercise, respond quickly to Go shapes, but resist responding to No-Go shapes. Go trials are more frequent, so they build a habit of responding that you must override. [1][6]

Green circle = Go Press Space or tap Respond.
Red square = No-Go Do not respond.
Press Start to begin.
