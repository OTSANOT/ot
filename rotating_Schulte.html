How to Play

1. Concentric Rotating Rings: Numbers (12, 22, 32, or 42) are distributed evenly across two or three concentric circular rings.

2. Ascending Search: Start at 1 and tap each number in ascending sequence until you complete the board.

3. Independent Motion: The rings rotate clockwise and counter-clockwise at different speeds, requiring continuous visual tracking and peripheral awareness.

4. Accuracy Matters: Every wrong tap flashes red and adds a +1.0 second penalty to your final score.
